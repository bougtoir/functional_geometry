# CTEP adversarial review — Phase 5

Role-play: CTEP handling editor plus two skeptical reviewers, written before packaging. Severity: CRITICAL (desk reject / scope-fatal), MAJOR (revision-level), MINOR.

## E-1. Editor scope screen

The journal's Clean Technology thrust explicitly covers "mathematical and computer-based methods and models for designing, analyzing, and measuring the cleanliness of products and processes." The paper is exactly that class: a screening model for source reduction. **Not a scope-fatal mismatch.** Risk remains that an editor reads "disposable products + consumer behavior" as insufficiently technological — mitigated by framing as a design-screening tool and by the reproducible computational pipeline.

## R1-CRIT-1. Novelty: "renamed eco-design with elementary arithmetic" — CRITICAL, answered

The estimand (material per service) is inherited from MIPS/material-efficiency work. What no cited prior study supplies: (i) explicit break-even boundaries mapping geometry reduction to behavioral/engineering failure conditions; (ii) the rate-vs-probability distinction for replacement (25.0% vs 20.0% at 20% shortening — a reviewer who conflates these is answered by two separate equations); (iii) demonstration that a *selectable* dimension can return zero saving (two-half-units boundary); (iv) integer-logistics discontinuity (360/540/600). The paper concedes inheritance everywhere and claims (B) integration + falsification framework. Desk-reject risk cannot be eliminated because the contribution is genuinely incremental; it is defensible, not dominant.

## R1-MAJ-1. No empirical validation — MAJOR, disclosed not removable

No human-use study; patent contrasts are weak focus-group evidence and are labeled as such; Monte Carlo distributions are configured, not fitted. The abstract, Methods 2.7, Discussion 4.8, captions, and Conclusion all state this. Fix applied: none needed — already explicit. Residual risk: a reviewer may still demand data; the answer is that the paper's claim is conditional-boundary, not effectiveness.

## R1-MAJ-2. "Simple arithmetic" — MAJOR, answered

Correct and intentional. The contribution is boundary location and falsification structure, not sophistication. Made explicit in Introduction and Conclusions.

## R2-MAJ-3. Integer-packing example is hypothetical — MAJOR, disclosed

Carton/cross-section are transparent assumptions demonstrating discontinuity, not a market estimate; unchanged-packaging (zero benefit) is included as a stress test. Stated in Methods 2.6, Results 3.5, Discussion 4.4.

## R2-MAJ-4. Replacement rate vs failure probability conflation — MAJOR, resolved

Two formulations are defined, separated, and given distinct break-evens (r* = s/(1−s) rate; p* = s probability at zero shifted burden). A 5% normalized container burden reduces both. The distinction is also preserved in the graphical abstract per QC.

## R2-MIN-1. Source claims / manufacturing feasibility — MINOR, resolved

Patent identities corrected earlier (Hicks, Friedrich, Olson/Georgia-Pacific, Steinhardt/P&G); converting penalties (web breaks, cross-tear, roll integrity) are explicit multiplicative penalties and discussion text, not asserted feasibility.

## R2-MIN-2. Overstatement checks — MINOR, pass

No claim of: population effectiveness, universal optimum width/length, full LCA, transport-emission reduction, patentability/FTO, universal PFAS presence. Verified by scan of rendered text plus prior claim audit (`config/claim_audit.json`).

## R2-MIN-3. "FGO" branding risk — MINOR, mitigated

FGO is labeled "study-specific analytical lens" in title, abstract, intro, and conclusion; a new-field claim is expressly disclaimed. Reviewers may still find the coinage unnecessary; the screening-framework title reduces reliance on the term.

## Editorial decision simulation

Most likely outcome if it passes the desk screen: **major revision** centered on empirical validation requests (cannot be satisfied with existing data — response letter would restate conditional claim) and requests for a real case calibration of packing/logistics. Probability of desk rejection is real (novelty judgment is editorial discretion) but the journal-fit argument is materially stronger than at JCLP.

**Verdict: CTEP remains GO.** No fatal flaw identified that can be fixed with existing evidence; none found unfixed that invalidates the conditional contribution.
