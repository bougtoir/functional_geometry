Date: 2026-10-18. Sources: `reports/CTEP_CLOSEST_PRIOR_STUDIES.csv` (each row DOI/publisher-verified 2026-09-24 via `reports/references_verified_final.csv`), `reports/cleaner_production_literature_report.md`, live CTEP listing (accessed 2026-10-18).

## Ecology position

The paper sits downstream of four established strands, all already cited:

1. **Material efficiency / service denominators** (Allwood 2011, 2013; Liedtke 2014 MIPS; van der Voet 2004). Established: material per service delivered; mass reduction alone insufficient. None supply a product-geometry screening instrument.
2. **Eco-design / circular design tools** (Bovea 2012; Ardente 2014; Bocken 2016; Geissdoerfer 2017). Established: geometry exists as one variable inside broad frameworks. None isolate geometric dimension change with behavioral and logistic boundary conditions.
3. **Consumables** (Willskytt 2021). Established: durable-product guidance does not transfer. Supports our case selection; supplies no quantitative tool.
4. **Rebound / compensation** (Castro 2022; Metic 2022; Lowe 2024). Established: rebound taxonomy. Our model *instantiates* compensation as explicit break-even terms rather than describing it qualitatively.

Adjacent empirical work (straw LCAs: Chitaka 2020; Zanghelini 2020; Moy 2021; Gutierrez 2019; thinkstep 2019) compares *materials*, not *dimensions* — our straw case adds geometry and accessory–container co-design they do not cover.

**CTEP-internal adjacency:** a scan of recent CTEP content (Springer journal listing, 2026-10-18) shows the journal is dominated by process engineering, waste management, and energy/climate topics; the nearest item is a sustainable-packaging-waste review (Yerraju et al., in press). No substantively competing CTEP paper was found; no CTEP citations will be padded into the reference list (Yerraju may be cited once only if a natural logistics/packaging hook is used).

## Hostile challenge, answered

*"This is established eco-design renamed and illustrated by elementary break-even arithmetic and hypothetical distributions."*

- The *estimand* (material per successful functional event) is inherited — acknowledged. The paper does not claim a new field.
- What prior work does **not** contain: (i) an explicit break-even mapping from geometric reduction to behavioral/engineering boundary conditions (longitudinal compensation ratio 1.20; replacement-rate 25.0% vs failure-probability 20.0% as two *distinct* formulations); (ii) dispensing-granularity analysis showing a center perforation can yield *zero* area saving under two-half use; (iii) the coupling of unit geometry to integer logistics (360/540/600 discontinuities); (iv) a declared observed-vs-assumed evidence separation with fixed-seed scenario Monte Carlo and falsification protocols. None of the 12 closest studies provide any of these as a usable pre-implementation screen.
- The arithmetic is deliberately elementary: the contribution is *where the boundaries sit and which missing measurements decide them*, not algorithmic sophistication. CTEP's stated scope explicitly covers "mathematical and computer-based methods and models for designing, analyzing, and measuring the cleanliness of products" — a screening framework is in-scope even without experimental data.

## Classification

**(B) Original integration and actionable falsification framework.** Not (A): no new algorithm and no new empirical result is claimed. Not (C): the integration yields explicit, falsifiable, cross-case boundaries that no cited prior work supplies.
