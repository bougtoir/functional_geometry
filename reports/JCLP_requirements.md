# Journal of Cleaner Production: current submission requirements

**Verified:** 2026-09-24; final policy recheck completed 2026-09-25
**Target:** *Journal of Cleaner Production* (JCLP; ISSN 0959-6526)
**Controlling source:** the live official JCLP Guide for Authors. Where generic Elsevier guidance or third-party summaries differ, the live journal-specific guide controls.

The final 2026-09-25 recheck of the official JCLP Guide for Authors and
Elsevier generative-AI policy found no submission-relevant change to the
requirements applied below. The current AI policy explicitly requires the
tool/service name, purpose, human oversight, and author responsibility; use of
AI in the research process must also be described in Methods. The manuscript
does both.

## Bottom line for this project

Submit the functional-geometry study as an **Original article**, not a Review article or Technical note. The official definition is a standard research paper of **6,000–8,000 words** in which hypotheses are tested and results reported. Review articles are commissioned and unsolicited reviews are not considered; Technical notes are approximately 3,000 words and concern new products/technologies. All contribution types are subject to an explicit **maximum of 50 references**.
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).

The proposed paper is plausibly in scope if framed and demonstrated as a **transdisciplinary cleaner-production, sustainable-products, and environmental/sustainability-assessment study**, rather than three isolated product ideas. JCLP defines cleaner production as preventing waste while increasing efficiency in energy, water, resources, and human capital; listed areas include cleaner-production technical processes, sustainable consumption, environmental/sustainability assessment, and sustainable products and services. The journal warns that good niche or disciplinary papers may be redirected to companion journals.
Sources: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors and https://www.elsevier.com/subject/environmental-science/journal-of-cleaner-production-family (accessed 2026-09-24).

For this project, the submission-ready default should therefore be:

- Original article, **6,000–8,000 words**; because the guide does not define whether references, tables, captions, or supplement count toward this range, keep the main manuscript text safely within the range and verify the Editorial Manager counter before submission. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).
- **Abstract ≤250 words**, concise and factual; it must state purpose, basic procedures, main findings (including statistical significance where applicable), and principal conclusions. The current official guide does **not** require labeled structured-abstract headings. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).
- **1–7 English keywords**; avoid multiword terms and abbreviations unless firmly established. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).
- **Highlights are required**: a separate editable file named to include “highlights,” containing **3–5 bullets, each ≤85 characters including spaces**. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).
- A **graphical abstract is encouraged, not required** by the current official guide. If supplied, upload it separately; minimum dimensions are **1328 × 531 pixels (width × height)** or proportionally larger, readable at 5 × 13 cm. JCLP lists TIFF, EPS, PDF, or Microsoft Office as preferred formats. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors and https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/tools-and-resources/graphical-abstract (accessed 2026-09-24).
- **No more than 50 references.** This directly overrides the project prompt’s provisional strategy of 80–100% of an assumed higher limit. Use a necessary-and-sufficient bibliography capped at 50. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).
- Deposit the public data, code, models, algorithms, protocols, and other research materials in a relevant repository and cite/link the deposit. JCLP applies Elsevier **Research Data Option C**: deposit and link are required, or the manuscript must explain why sharing is impossible. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors and https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/tools-and-resources/research-data/data-guidelines (accessed 2026-09-24).

## Verified detailed requirements

### Scope and article types

JCLP is an international, transdisciplinary journal covering cleaner production, environmental research, and sustainability research and practice. Cleaner production is expressly prevention-oriented. Its public scope includes cleaner-production processes, sustainable development, sustainable consumption, environmental/sustainability assessment, sustainable products/services, corporate sustainability, education, and governance/policy.
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).

Accepted contribution types listed in the current guide:

1. **Original article:** standard research paper, 6,000–8,000 words, testing hypotheses and reporting results.
2. **Review article:** state-of-the-art review with significant and novel hypotheses/perspectives; commissioned by Editors-in-Chief; unsolicited review submissions are not considered, though authors may contact the Editors-in-Chief with an idea.
3. **Technical note:** approximately 3,000 words, reporting new products/technologies relevant to cleaner production; illustrations allowed, company logos prohibited.
4. **Letter to the Editor:** clarifies/responds to a previously published paper or raises journal-relevant questions.

All types have a maximum of 50 references.
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).

### Peer review, title page, and anonymization

JCLP uses **single-anonymized review**, normally with at least two independent reviewers after editorial screening. Under Elsevier’s definition, reviewer identities are hidden from authors, but author identities are visible to reviewers. Therefore, authors should **not anonymize the manuscript** unless the submission portal gives a contrary manuscript-specific instruction.
Sources: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors and https://www.elsevier.com/reviewer/what-is-peer-review (accessed 2026-09-24).

A title page is required and must contain:

- concise, informative article title, avoiding abbreviations/formulae unless established;
- all author given and family names in submission-system order;
- complete affiliations where work was performed, including postal address and country, keyed by lower-case superscripts;
- identified corresponding author and published email address;
- present/permanent address footnotes where applicable, while retaining the research affiliation as the main address.

The public guide requires a title page but does **not** clearly state that it must be uploaded as a file separate from the manuscript. Because review is single-anonymized, a separate blinded-manuscript/title-page pair is not logically required by the stated review model. Confirm the available file designations in Editorial Manager at submission.
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).

### Manuscript and source-file formats

Editable source files are required for the entire submission, including text, figures, tables, and text graphics. Use **DOC/DOCX** for Word or **TEX** for LaTeX; PDF alone is not an acceptable source file. Word manuscripts must use a single-column layout; two columns are allowed only for LaTeX. Remove non-scientific underlining/strikethrough and run spelling/grammar checks.
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).

Number manuscript sections and subsections (1, 1.1, 1.1.1, etc.); do not number the abstract. Use footnotes sparingly. Put acknowledgements in a separate section immediately before the references, not on the title page or in a title footnote.
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).

The corresponding author must supply a **CRediT author-contribution statement** using applicable roles such as Conceptualization, Data curation, Formal analysis, Methodology, Software, Validation, Visualization, Writing–original draft, and Writing–review and editing.
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).

### Tables, figures, equations, and supplement

Tables:

- must be editable text, not images;
- may be placed near relevant text or on separate pages at the manuscript end;
- must be cited and numbered consecutively in order of appearance;
- require captions and notes beneath the table;
- should avoid vertical rules and cell shading;
- should not duplicate text or other displays.

Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).

Figures/artwork:

- must be cited and consecutively numbered;
- each figure should normally be uploaded as a **separate file** with a logical name such as `Figure_1`;
- text graphics may be embedded, including in LaTeX;
- every artwork item requires a concise caption explaining symbols and abbreviations;
- vector drawings: EPS or PDF, with fonts embedded or text converted to graphics;
- photographs: TIFF/JPG/PNG, minimum 300 dpi, minimum width 1063 px single-column or 2244 px full-page;
- bitmapped line drawings: TIFF/JPG/PNG, minimum 1000 dpi, minimum width 3543 px single-column or 7480 px full-page;
- charts or large/shaded tables: XLS/XLSX;
- annotated images: PPT/PPTX, with legible annotations and embedded images at least 300 dpi;
- do not supply low-resolution artwork, disproportionately small labels, or unrelated multi-image composites;
- ensure color figures remain accessible to readers with impaired color vision.

Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors and https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/author/artwork-and-media-instructions/artwork-formats-checklist (accessed 2026-09-24).

Equations must be editable, not images; variables should be italicized; display equations should be consecutively numbered in citation order.
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).

Supplementary files must be submitted with the manuscript, cited in the text, and accompanied by concise captions. They are published as received and are not typeset or reformatted. After initial submission they can be added/replaced only during revision.
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).

The guide states no explicit maximum number of main figures or tables. The proposed 5–6 main figures is therefore not prohibited, but remains an editorial-design choice rather than a verified journal limit.
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).

### References

JCLP uses an **author–year** style, not Vancouver numbering:

- one author: surname and year;
- two authors: both surnames and year;
- three or more: first surname plus “et al.” and year;
- reference list alphabetical, then chronological where needed;
- journal names abbreviated using LTWA;
- include DOIs where available;
- cite datasets and software as references, using persistent identifiers;
- identify dataset references with `[dataset]` immediately before the reference entry;
- web references must include the full URL and last-accessed date;
- clearly identify preprints and prefer the peer-reviewed version once available.

Every in-text citation must appear in the list and vice versa; every reference must be a real source. The hard journal cap is 50 references.
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).

### Declarations, ethics, funding, and AI

**Competing interests:** all authors must use Elsevier’s declarations tool. The resulting declaration document must be uploaded as DOC/DOCX; signatures are not required. Authors with nothing to declare select “I have nothing to declare.” Employment, consultancies, ownership, honoraria, expert testimony, patents, grants/funding, and journal roles are examples of potentially relevant interests.
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).

**Funding:** disclose every financial-support source and the sponsor’s role in design, data collection, analysis/interpretation, writing, and submission. Use standardized funder names and grant numbers. If unfunded, the guide recommends: “This research did not receive any specific grant from funding agencies in the public, commercial, or not-for-profit sectors.”
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).

**Ethics:** the JCLP guide incorporates Elsevier’s Publishing Ethics Policy. If work involves human participants, their data/material, or identifiable information, Elsevier requires compliance with relevant law/institutional guidance, appropriate committee approval, and a manuscript statement containing approval date and reference number (or an applicable exemption explanation). Animal research must follow Elsevier’s animal-research policy. For the proposed public-data modelling/scenario study, do not invent an ethics approval; state that no human-participant or animal experiment was conducted if accurate, and separately describe future proposed trials as protocols not performed research.
Sources: https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/publishing-ethics and https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/research-ethics (accessed 2026-09-24).

**Generative AI:** manuscript-preparation use must be disclosed in a new section immediately before the references, naming the tool/service, purpose, human review/editing, and author responsibility. Basic grammar/spelling/reference checking and disability-assistive tools used only for accessibility do not require disclosure. AI cannot be an author. AI used as part of the research must also be described in Methods.
Sources: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors and https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals (accessed 2026-09-24).

Elsevier’s current image policy permits AI assistance for some explanatory images (e.g., flow charts, decision trees, timelines, schematics, and workflow diagrams) with human oversight and disclosure in both the caption and general AI statement. Accuracy and originality remain the authors’ responsibility. Avoid generative AI for scientific/data images; generate analytical figures directly from code.
Sources: https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals and https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).

### Data and code

JCLP’s Option C policy is stronger than a merely encouraged data statement. Authors are required to:

1. deposit research data in a relevant repository;
2. cite and link the dataset in the article;
3. if sharing is impossible, state why.

The guide defines research data broadly to include observations/experiments, software, code, models, algorithms, protocols, and methods. For this project, archive the public cleaned data, source/assumption registries, analysis code, pinned environment, generated outputs needed for verification, and a release corresponding exactly to the submitted manuscript. Give the archive a persistent identifier and cite it as a dataset/software reference.
Sources: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors and https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/tools-and-resources/research-data/data-guidelines (accessed 2026-09-24).

### Submission checklist

Before submission, the official checklist requires:

- one designated corresponding author with complete, current contact details;
- all files uploaded, including artwork, videos, supplement, tables, footnotes, and captions;
- spelling and grammar checks completed;
- complete two-way correspondence between text citations and reference list;
- permissions obtained for all third-party copyrighted material, including web material;
- for open access, all authors understand responsibility for an APC if accepted, subject to institutional/funder coverage.

The submission system converts uploaded files to one PDF for review, but editable source files remain required for production.
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors and https://service.elsevier.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/10707/supporthub/publishing/role/author/ (accessed 2026-09-24).

### Publication model

JCLP is a **hybrid (“supports open access”) journal**:

- subscription publication has no open-access publication fee;
- gold open access is optional and does not affect peer review or acceptance;
- the live official page displayed an APC of **USD 4,580 excluding taxes** for all article types on 2026-09-24, before individualized agreements/discounts;
- listed OA licenses are CC BY, CC BY-NC, and CC BY-NC-ND;
- subscription articles permit accepted-manuscript self-archiving after a **24-month embargo** under the stated policy.

APCs are mutable; re-check the live page when choosing the publication route and again at acceptance.
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/open-access-options (accessed 2026-09-24).

## Contradictions and unresolved items

1. **Reference strategy:** the project prompt contemplated 80–100% of an unknown maximum. The current official JCLP guide explicitly caps all contributions at **50 references**. The manuscript and bibliography must be designed around 50, not 80–100. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).
2. **Abstract:** some third-party 2026 pages claim a 300-word structured abstract, while other third-party summaries claim 250 words. The live official guide says **≤250 words** and does not prescribe structured headings. Follow the official 250-word rule. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).
3. **Graphical abstract:** third-party pages call it required. The live official guide says **“encouraged,”** not required. It is nevertheless strategically valuable for this geometrical framework. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).
4. **Separate title-page file:** a title page is required, but the public guide does not say whether it is a separate upload. Single-anonymized review means author identities need not be removed. Confirm file categories in the live submission form. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors and https://submit.elsevier.com/JCLEPRO (accessed 2026-09-24).
5. **Cover letter:** the public Guide for Authors and displayed checklist do not explicitly state that a cover letter is mandatory. Prepare one because it is useful and commonly available as a submission item, but do not label it a verified mandatory component until the submission form confirms it. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors and https://submit.elsevier.com/JCLEPRO (accessed 2026-09-24).
6. **Word-count inclusions:** “6,000–8,000 words … with tables, illustrations and references” is stated, but the public guide does not define exactly which components the counter includes. Keep the main text conservatively within range and inspect the portal’s validation behavior. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).
7. **Figure/table quantity:** no explicit number cap was found in the public guide. Use only displays necessary to evaluate the cleaner-production proposition. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).
8. **APC discrepancy:** a text-search cache returned USD 4,620, whereas the live official page rendered **USD 4,580** on 2026-09-24. Treat USD 4,580 as the current observed value but regard all APC amounts as volatile and re-check before any financial decision. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/open-access-options (accessed 2026-09-24).
9. **Study-specific reporting checklist:** no JCLP-specific CONSORT/STROBE/PRISMA requirement was found for this modelling/scenario-analysis design. Apply reporting standards only where substantively applicable and do not characterize the work as a trial or full LCA without meeting those methods. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).

## Recommended submission file set

1. Editable main manuscript in DOCX or TEX, with title page, ≤250-word abstract, 1–7 keywords, numbered sections, acknowledgements, CRediT, funding, competing-interest text as appropriate, data/code availability, ethics/non-applicability statement, and generative-AI declaration before references. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).
2. Separate editable highlights file: 3–5 bullets, each ≤85 characters. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).

4. Each main figure as a separate compliant file, plus captions in the manuscript or a caption section. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).
5. Editable tables in manuscript or separate editable pages/files as the portal allows. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).
6. Supplementary material, submitted simultaneously and cited in the manuscript. https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-cleaner-production/publish/guide-for-authors (accessed 2026-09-24).
