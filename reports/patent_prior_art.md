# Patent prior-art report: selectable-width and geometry-reduced tissue products

**Role:** Patent prior art
**Access date for all web records:** 2026-09-24
**Scope:** Toilet paper or adjacent tissue/paper products involving longitudinal or central perforation, divided width, selectable portions, multiple tear directions, reduced width/shape, or related dispensing. This is a technical literature search, **not** a patentability, validity, infringement, claim-construction, or freedom-to-operate opinion.

## Executive finding

The physical concepts at the center of the proposed toilet-paper case are not safely claimable as unprecedented. Direct records disclose: (i) a toilet/wrapping-paper web divided into longitudinal series by vertical and transverse weakness lines as early as 1891 ([US453003A](https://patents.google.com/patent/US453003A/en)); (ii) toilet paper formed as two side-by-side strips joined by a common central perforation, plus oblique and alternative tear geometries ([WO2003026472A1, Google](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2003026472A1/en); [WIPO](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2003026472)); (iii) narrower toilet-paper sheets whose effective folded width is restored by angled or indented side geometry ([WO2010109404, WIPO](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2010109404); [WO2015112867, WIPO](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2015112867)); and (iv) toilet-paper widths of 2–3.6 inches combined with a conventional-holder adapter ([WO2021222474, WIPO](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2021222474); [US20230165413A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20230165413A1/en)).

Adjacent paper-towel art is even more explicit about consumer-selectable increments: full, half, or quarter sheets produced by cross-machine and machine-direction perforations ([WO2016191420, WIPO](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2016191420); [US9918595B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9918595B2/en)); generic bidirectionally perforated rolled webs and center-pull dispensing ([US9918596B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9918596B2/en)); and a central longitudinal perforation combined with orthogonal secondary perforations having different tear forces ([US10390662B1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10390662B1/en)).

Accordingly, the paper should not present center-perforated/selectable-half-width toilet paper, reduced-width toilet paper, or bidirectional tear geometry as the scientific novelty. A defensible contribution would instead be a reproducible cleaner-production evaluation of previously disclosed geometry classes: functional-unit denominators, behavior/compensation, break-even conditions, manufacturing and compatibility constraints, packaging/logistics consequences, lifecycle-informed burden, uncertainty, and cross-product generalization. The cited patents disclose structures and intended functions, but do not by themselves establish commercial adoption, user equivalence, material saving per successful use, or environmental benefit ([WO2003026472A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2003026472A1/en); [WO2021222474](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2021222474); [WO2016191420](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2016191420)).

## Novelty consequences for the cleaner-production paper

1. **Remove product-invention novelty claims.** Do not claim that a longitudinally divided toilet roll, central perforation, selectable half-width, multiple tear directions, narrow roll, or compatibility adapter is first or unprecedented; each is substantially disclosed in the records above ([US453003A](https://patents.google.com/patent/US453003A/en); [WO2003026472A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2003026472A1/en); [WO2021222474](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2021222474); [WO2016191420](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2016191420)).

2. **Describe the geometries as evaluated intervention classes.** Appropriate language is “we quantitatively evaluate previously disclosed or technically analogous geometry strategies” rather than “we invent/propose a novel center-perforated roll” ([WO2003026472A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2003026472A1/en); [US9918595B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9918595B2/en)).

3. **Locate novelty in cleaner-production evidence, if the literature review supports it.** The potentially differentiating contribution is a cross-case quantitative framework that links geometry to material per successful functional use, behavior/rebound, failure/replacement, manufacturing and holder compatibility, packaging/transport, lifecycle-informed coefficients, uncertainty, and break-even boundaries. Patent disclosures state intended structures and advantages but do not supply that integrated environmental-performance evidence ([WO2015112867](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2015112867); [WO2021222474](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2021222474); [US8741410B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8741410B2/en)).

4. **Treat behavior as part of the estimand, not an afterthought.** The partitionable-towel family links perforation strengths to user-selectable full/half/quarter portions, while dispensing art explicitly identifies over-pulling as a consumption problem; therefore, realized saving requires compensation and separation-success analysis ([US9918595B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9918595B2/en); [US8741410B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8741410B2/en)).

5. **Separate technical feasibility from environmental efficacy.** Manufacturing records show routes to complex or bidirectional perforation, but they do not prove that a toilet-paper implementation preserves adequacy, acceptance, production yield, or environmental benefit per use ([WO2010076689](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2010076689); [US8757058B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8757058B2/en)).

6. **Avoid converting patent language into empirical evidence.** Assertions in abstracts such as “maintaining wiping performance” or “reducing consumption” are disclosure statements, not substitutes for user data, bench testing, market deployment evidence, or lifecycle assessment ([WO2010109404](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2010109404); [WO2015112867](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2015112867); [WO2021222474](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2021222474)).

### Suggested manuscript wording

> Longitudinally partitioned and geometry-reduced tissue products have substantial patent precedent, including early longitudinal/transverse weakness-line toilet paper, centrally joined toilet-paper strips, reduced-width toilet-paper geometries, and bidirectionally perforated paper towels. We therefore treat these configurations as intervention classes rather than product inventions and investigate a different question: under explicit functional-unit, behavioral-compensation, manufacturing, compatibility, packaging, logistics, and uncertainty assumptions, when does geometry optimization reduce material throughput and modeled environmental burden? ([US453003A](https://patents.google.com/patent/US453003A/en); [WO2003026472A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2003026472A1/en); [WO2016191420](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2016191420); [WO2021222474](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2021222474))

## Search method, verification, and unresolved uncertainty

- Searches covered exact and adjacent terms for toilet paper/tissue, longitudinal or machine-direction perforation, central/common perforation, divided/partitionable width, full/half/quarter sheets, customized tear force, reduced width, angled/indented sides, overconsumption, and related roll dispensing. Relevant records were followed through cited/citing and PCT-family links in [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/) and [WIPO PATENTSCOPE](https://patentscope.wipo.int/).
- Google Patents full-text pages used in this report were saved locally with checksums. WIPO bibliographic pages were verified interactively and preserved as screenshots because direct command-line requests returned HTTP 403. Acquisition outcomes, file sizes, SHA-256 hashes, source URLs, and failure notes are recorded in `data/raw/patents/acquisition_ledger.csv`.
- Direct [Espacenet](https://worldwide.espacenet.com/) access was blocked by a Cloudflare human-verification page in the research environment, and EPO OPS returned an anonymous-quota HTTP 403. Espacenet publication URLs are therefore supplied for record access, but Espacenet bibliographic content was not independently relied on where Google/WIPO verification is stated.
- WIPO explicitly cautions that publication in PATENTSCOPE does not express an opinion on legal status ([example record and disclaimer](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2003026472)). Google likewise labels its legal-status and assignee fields as assumptions/non-legal conclusions on the cited patent pages (for example [US9918595B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9918595B2/en)). Current enforceability, ownership, national-phase survival, claim scope, and prosecution status remain outside this report.
- This search is high-confidence for the located concepts but is not guaranteed exhaustive across all jurisdictions, non-English terminology, unpublished applications, continuations/divisionals, or imperfectly indexed historical documents. A legal opinion would require jurisdiction-specific family review, official registers, claims analysis, and professional counsel.

## Bottom line

Confirmed prior art materially narrows the paper’s novelty claim: the toilet-paper geometries are examples to be **evaluated**, not inventions to be claimed as new. The paper can still make a potentially meaningful cleaner-production contribution if it demonstrates, rather than assumes, functional equivalence and net benefit; explicitly models rebound and failure; distinguishes mass saving from environmental impact; and generalizes a quantitative geometry-optimization method beyond the already-disclosed product configurations ([WO2003026472A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2003026472A1/en); [WO2016191420](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2016191420); [WO2021222474](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2021222474)).
