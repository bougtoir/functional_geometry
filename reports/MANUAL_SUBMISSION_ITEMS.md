# Manual submission items

These are the only unresolved author- or portal-dependent items. The build does
not infer or fabricate them.

## Replace the conspicuous `AUTHOR ACTION` fields

- `Manuscript.docx` and `Title_page.docx`: add every author's institutional
  affiliation; add the corresponding author's full postal address and current
  email.
- `Manuscript.docx`, Funding: confirm whether funding existed. If none, insert
  “This research did not receive any specific grant from funding agencies in
  the public, commercial, or not-for-profit sectors.” If funded, insert exact
  funder names, grant identifiers, and sponsor roles.
- `Manuscript.docx`, Competing interests: complete Elsevier's declarations
  tool, upload its DOC/DOCX output, and insert the author-confirmed statement.
- `Manuscript.docx`, CRediT: confirm and insert each author's final roles.
- `Manuscript.docx`, Data and code availability: create a release for the exact
  submitted commit, archive it with a persistent DOI or identifier, and insert
  that identifier.
- `Cover_letter.docx`: insert the submission date, affiliation, and
  corresponding-author email; confirm originality, exclusive consideration,
  and approval by every author.

## Confirm in the submission system

  contact details, and any author-confirmed ORCID identifiers.
- Confirm the ethics statement remains accurate: this study used public
  documents and hypothetical scenarios and did not enroll participants or use
  identifiable personal data.
- Confirm the generative-AI statement lists all AI tools actually used. The
  present statement identifies Devin by Cognition AI, its purposes, human
  review/editing, and author responsibility.
- Select `Original article`; enter required classifications, keywords, editor
  comments, and suggested or opposed reviewers.
- Confirm whether the portal requests a separate title-page file and cover
  letter.
- Choose subscription or open access and confirm any applicable APC or
  institutional agreement.
- Review and approve the system-generated submission PDF.
