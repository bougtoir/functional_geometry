## Aims & scope fit

Official overview: "publishes research on cleaner products, processes, and environmental policy strategies." The Clean Technology thrust "addresses the science and engineering of clean technologies" and "examines **mathematical and computer-based methods and models for designing, analyzing, and measuring the cleanliness of products and processes**."

The paper is a model-based, service-normalized screening framework for geometric source reduction in disposable products — i.e. a mathematical/computer-based method for analysing cleanliness (material demand per successful use) of products. Scope fit: **defensible**.

## Article types (official list)

## Format requirements (from submission guidelines, accessed 2026-10-18)

- Abstract: **150–250 words**.
- Keywords: **4–6**.
- Citations: **author–year (name and year in parentheses)**; reference list alphabetized by first author; DOIs as full `https://doi.org/...` links. → JCLP numbered style must be converted to Springer name–date; this is a real formatting change.
- Headings: ≤3 displayed levels.
- Title page: title, author info (unaffiliated authors: city + country only unless email requested), acknowledgments on title page.
- Editable source files required at submission (docx or LaTeX) — else not considered.
- Graphical abstract **mandatory at first submission**.
- Footnotes allowed; endnotes not.
- Statements & Declarations: Author Contribution + Competing Interests entered via submission interface; Data Availability Statement **mandatory for all original research**; LLM use must be documented (Methods or suitable alternative); AI-assisted copy editing need not be declared. Our existing generative-AI disclosure should be retained.
- No page limit stated for original research papers; no mandatory colour charge mentioned (Springer standard: colour free online). No page charges listed anywhere in the guidelines.
- Source: https://link.springer.com/journal/10098/submission-guidelines (accessed 2026-10-18).
