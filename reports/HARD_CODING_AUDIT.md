# Hard-coding audit

## Scope

The audit traced quantitative claims in the abstract, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusions, tables, figures, graphical abstract, and supplement to the current configuration, processed data, or generated analysis outputs.

## Controls

- `results/manuscript_values.csv` is regenerated by `scripts/run_analysis.py`.
- The registry records a value identifier, numerical value, unit, source file, method, evidence status, and manuscript location.
- `scripts/build_submission.py` resolves manuscript placeholders only from that registry and fails on a missing identifier.
- The graphical abstract reads its width and straw values from the same registry.
- Tables and analytical figures are regenerated from `config/scenarios.json` and processed data.
- `scripts/run_qc.py` fails when a registry source is missing, rendered placeholders remain, or the reference/claim audits are incomplete.

## Remaining literal numbers

Literal values that remain in manuscript prose are definitions, cited measurements, equation constants, or explicitly configured scenario-distribution parameters. They are not estimated study results. The post-audit result claims listed above are registry-driven so that rebuilding after a data or configuration change updates the manuscript and submission graphics.
