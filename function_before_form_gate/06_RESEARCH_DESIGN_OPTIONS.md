## Gate E — study routes

### E1 Conceptual synthesis
- Question: integrate design methodology and DfS around function-first. Novelty: none beyond S01–S04, S17. Evidence: available. Identification: n/a. Threats: reviewer D1–D3. Minimum study: literature paper. Effort: low. **Verdict: NO-GO.**

### E2 Comparative published cases
- Question: in published LCAs, do form/delivery changes outperform material substitution? Evidence available: case LCAs (S35–S37), but each answers a different functional unit and system boundary. Identification: none — case selection is driven by what was studied and published; results conflict by case (EPS lowest in S35; R-PET lowest in S36). Threats: cherry-picking, publication bias, non-comparable functional units, attributional vs consequential mixing. Minimum defensible study: pre-registered systematic review with fixed inclusion rules and functional-unit harmonisation. Effort: high; contribution would duplicate existing packaging LCA reviews. **Verdict: NO-GO** as an independent contribution (usable only as background for E3 inventories).

### E3 Randomised three-arm framing experiment — only surviving route
- **Question (narrowest defensible):** With identical time, information and functional requirement, does framing a brief as (A) material substitution, (B) form-preserving redesign, or (C) form-unconstrained functional need change (i) the screening-LCA impact per functional unit of the concept each participant selects as final, (ii) the proportion of final concepts that pass a pre-specified functional-equivalence and feasibility screen, and (iii) feature similarity to the reference product?
- **Novelty:** environmental endpoint + framing *level* manipulation; not found in screened literature. Unverified risk: Collado-Ruiz 2010.
- **Design essentials:** ≥2–3 reference products (not only a spoon) to avoid single-case artefacts; participants stratified by expertise (students vs professionals; S09 shows experts also fixate); the only difference between arms is the framing paragraph; manipulation checks; blinded assessors.
- **Functional equivalence:** pre-specified function list per product (e.g., for cutlery: transfer of defined food types, contact time, hygiene, user acceptance proxy); concepts failing are recorded, not discarded, and reported as an outcome.
- **Environmental outcome:** blinded screening LCA using pre-built archetype inventories (material, mass class, process, use cycles, end-of-life) and a fixed functional unit; uncertainty reported as ranges; sensitivity for reuse counts (S35 shows break-even is decisive). This protocol does not yet exist and must be piloted for inter-assessor agreement before any main study (D10).
- **Validity threats:** endpoint validity (Faludi 2020 judged it too uncertain for vague ideas); rater bias; arm C producing service/system concepts outside LCA archetypes; demand effects; ecological validity (lab vs practice); multiple products/outcomes → pre-registration and correction.
- **Costs/feasibility without industry:** feasible with design/engineering students (course-embedded or paid), plus one LCA-competent assessor pair. Requires **ethics approval** and **new human-subject data**. No sample size can be stated until a pilot provides variance; no effect size was assumed.
- **Effort:** protocol + ethics: weeks; pilot: one cohort; main study: one academic term or more.
- **Verdict: CONDITIONAL GO** (conditions in `08_FINAL_NOVELTY_GATE.md`).

### E4 Retrospective patents / specifications / EPDs / product histories
- Question: how often do environmental redesigns change material only vs form/function? Evidence: EPDs and patents exist but record outcomes, not the alternatives considered; no counterfactual; selection (firms that publish EPDs), publication and survivorship bias; classification of "form change" is subjective. Could estimate prevalence (Gate C, C1) but not effect. **Verdict: NO-GO** as main route; possible later descriptive add-on.

### E5 Bounded-search / path-dependence decision model
- Equivalence check before implementation: with the same objective and feasible set, exhaustive search makes order irrelevant (D4). With bounded search, the model reduces to satisficing/sequential search with early commitment — standard bounded-rationality and path-dependence results (Arthur 1989; fixation). Any interior result would depend on assumed search costs and fixation parameters with no empirical calibration — the same failure mode as FUGD. **Verdict: NO-GO** (not implemented, per instruction).
