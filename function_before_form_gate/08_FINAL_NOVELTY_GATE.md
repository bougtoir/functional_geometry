# 08 — Final Novelty Gate

## Verdict: **CONDITIONAL GO — Empirical Research (single narrow route E3 only)**

**NO-GO** for conceptual synthesis (E1), Perspective/critical paper (B5), published-case comparison (E2), retrospective patents/EPDs (E4) and a sequencing/decision model (E5).

The principle "function is the requirement, form is a hypothesis, material is a choice" is **established design theory and ecodesign doctrine**, not a new contribution. The only thing not found in the screened literature is an **empirical test** of whether brief framing level changes the **environmental performance** of the designs people select. That test does not exist yet, needs new human-subject data, and has an unvalidated outcome measure. Hence "conditional": if any condition below fails, the verdict becomes NO-GO.

## Direct answers

- **Is there a publishable contribution beyond established design theory/ecodesign?** Not at present. A conditional one exists only as an unexecuted experiment with an environmental endpoint.
- **Is the principle established?** Yes (design methodology + LCA functional units + DfS levels).
- **Is substitution bias empirically demonstrated?** Only for consumers (material-type heuristics) and as case-level burden shifting. **Not established** for designers or firms.
- **Is sequencing independently testable?** Not as optimisation (tautological under exhaustive search). Only as a bounded-effort framing effect, which is the experiment E3.
- **Is an empirical study required?** Yes. Without it there is no contribution.

## Conditions for the CONDITIONAL GO (all required, in order)

1. **Prior-art closure:** read Collado-Ruiz & Ostad-Ahmad-Ghorabi 2010 in full and run a Scopus/Web of Science/Google Scholar search (brief framing × LCA/environmental assessment of concepts). If a comparable study exists → NO-GO or replication framing.
2. **Endpoint protocol:** a written, blinded screening-LCA protocol for concept-level designs (archetype inventories, fixed functional units, functional-equivalence/feasibility screen, uncertainty ranges), piloted for inter-assessor agreement. If agreement is poor → NO-GO.
3. **Access and ethics:** a participant population (students and/or practitioners) and ethics approval; an LCA-competent assessor pair.
4. **Pre-registration** of two-sided hypotheses, arms, products (≥2), outcomes and analysis before data collection.

## Minimum next step
Condition 1 (prior-art closure) — no participants, no manuscript. It needs institutional database access and the Collado-Ruiz 2010 full text, which this VM could not obtain.
