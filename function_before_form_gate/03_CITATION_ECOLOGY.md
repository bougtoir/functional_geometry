## Observations

1. The fixation community and the ecodesign community overlap mainly through a small number of works (Collado-Ruiz 2010; Faludi et al. 2020; Devanathan et al. 2010). The bridge is thin; this is where the only gap sits.
2. A purely conceptual paper would sit in the most crowded part of the ecology (DfS evolution, PSS, circular design) where highly cited syntheses already exist. That is consistent with the earlier CTEP/JCLP desk rejections, although those concerned different formulations.
3. Fixation research itself questions whether measured feature repetition is "always bad" (Crilly & Cardoso 2017, full text). An environmental endpoint would answer a question the fixation community has raised, which is a legitimate entry point — but only with outcome data.
