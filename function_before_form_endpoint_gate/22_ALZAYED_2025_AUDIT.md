**Statistical notes.**
- With n = 30 split into two groups and multiple Mann–Whitney tests (Table 2, 330), power is very limited.
- The null result cannot be read as equivalence.
- Excluding participants who failed the manipulation check departs from ITT.

**Overlap classification: Substantial overlap** in paradigm: a brief/goal manipulation, one final idea per participant, and an environmental rating as outcome. **Distinct** in endpoint: no LCA, no FE gate, no uncertainty model. This leaves E3's endpoint claim (25) intact, but it removes "final selected concept" as a differentiator.
