## Outcome structure

1. **Functional adequacy (all randomised participants, ITT):** the FE category per participant (a missing concept counts as FAIL). Compared by arm with an ordinal model. This is a valid randomised comparison.
2. **Environmental impact of evaluable (FE-PASS) concepts:** **descriptive only**. Conditioning on FE-PASS selects a post-treatment subgroup (principal-stratification problem), so differences are not reported as causal effects.
3. **Primary joint outcome:** the pre-specified hierarchical ranking (18 §3): FE category first, then discernible impact difference, then tie. Pairs are compared across arms as a win-probability / win-ratio. It uses all participants and keeps the randomised comparison intact.
4. **Bounds:** optional sensitivity analysis giving worst/best-case bounds for the impact effect among always-evaluable participants, under monotonicity assumptions (stated as assumptions).

No effect size or sample size is calculated; both await the pilot's variance and agreement estimates.
