## NO EQUIVALENT FOLLOW-UP IDENTIFIED IN THE SEARCHED SOURCES

No published results of the experiments announced in CIIP 2012 were found. No study in this research line measures environmental impact per functional unit of concepts generated under manipulated conditions. The line splits into:
- (a) creativity effects of information;
- (b) early-stage impact-estimation and functional-unit methods.

E3 would combine the two strands, which the group itself pointed to (p. 494 of the 2010 paper). **This does not establish that no such study exists.** TESEO, Scopus/WoS and Spanish-language venues (e.g. CIIP proceedings 2013–2016) were not searched (24).
