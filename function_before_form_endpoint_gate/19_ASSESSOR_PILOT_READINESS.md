# 19 — Assessor Pilot Readiness

**ASSESSOR RELIABILITY — NOT TESTED.** No assessor has been recruited, and no rating has been produced or simulated. Agreement between two runs of the same script is not reliability evidence and is not used.

## 1. Pilot scope (methodological, no design-framing participants)

The pilot tests the *endpoint only*, on **documented** product concepts, with real assessors. It involves no randomised brief and no design participants.

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Concept set | 30–50 per task. Source: real product alternatives documented in the literature or on the market for the FU (single-use, reusable, edible/integrated, deposit/system variants). Each is rewritten into the 06 SCD template at concept-level detail by a non-assessor. Synthetic concepts may be used only to debug tools, labelled SYNTHETIC, and are excluded from reliability statistics |
| Reference cases | Concepts for which published full LCAs exist with a compatible FU, used for benchmark validity. Candidates come from the prior gate's searches (t05–t07: single-use alternatives, cutlery, packaging LCAs). **None has yet been checked for FU compatibility or inventory transparency** |
| Assessors | ≥ 2 independent LCA-competent assessors (≥ 1 completed product LCA), plus 1 adjudicator used only after the independent scores are locked |
| Instructions | 06 §3: FE first and locked, then archetype mapping, then the fixed tool, then lock |
| Blinding | Assessors are blind to concept source and to the other assessor. Benchmark results are hidden until the scores are locked |
| Independent inventory construction | Each assessor maps SCD fields to the frozen archetype library independently. No shared worksheet |
| Missing information | Archetype defaults with an `assumed` flag; the low-information label (05 §7) |
| Agreement measures | 07 §1: κ and AC1 (FE); ICC(2,1) absolute agreement on log impact; Kendall τ-b (rank); pairwise-dominance agreement; Bland–Altman (log ratio) |
| Benchmark comparison | Rank agreement (τ-b) and the log ratio of screening vs published LCA for the reference cases |
| Sensitivity | 18 §4 rank-stability share; per-assessor sensitivity to defaults |
| Pre-specified failure criteria | Frozen before the pilot. The endpoint fails if (i) the ICC lower 95% bound < 0.75 *and* the τ-b lower bound < 0.6; or (ii) the FE AC1 < 0.7 or κ < 0.6; or (iii) the benchmark τ-b lower bound < 0.6; or (iv) the rank-stability share is below the 18 §4 threshold. Any failure means **NO-GO for the E3 endpoint** in that task |
| Time cost | Recorded per concept (a feasibility outcome) |
