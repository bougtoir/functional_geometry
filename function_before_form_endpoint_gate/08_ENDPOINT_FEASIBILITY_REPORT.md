## 2. Is the endpoint defensible in principle?

**Yes, conditionally**, for the following reasons:
- Concept-stage screening LCA of repository-generated concepts has been benchmarked against real products (Bohm et al. 2010, AB).
- Function-level impact estimation (Devanathan 2010; Arlitt et al. 2016) and quantified early-LCA uncertainty (Kota & Chakrabarti 2010) exist.
- Faludi 2020's objection ("too subjective and too highly uncertain", PDF p. 10) applied to *large pools of vague Post-it ideas* assessed without structure. E3 changes three conditions:
  - only **one final concept per participant** is scored;
  - it is **elaborated into a fixed template**;
  - **archetype defaults and uncertainty are explicit**.

  Whether this is enough is an empirical question, and it is not yet answered.

**Residual risks:**
1. Uncertainty from uses-per-unit (u) and loss rate (r) can dominate reusable-versus-single-use comparisons. If it does, arm differences will sit inside the uncertainty band, and the study can only report "not discernible".
2. System/service concepts (e.g. return schemes) need infrastructure shares that early concepts rarely specify. This may bias assumed-default rates by arm. Arm C is the likely affected arm, because it invites system concepts.
3. Single-indicator (GWP) results can reverse under other indicators.

## 3. Can a pilot be run without new human-subject framing experiments?

**Yes.** The pilot tests only the assessment protocol:
- **Material:** documented real product alternatives for the chosen task. These are single-use plastic, wood/fibre and reusable options, and edible or system alternatives where published LCAs exist. Each is transcribed into the SCD template at *concept-level detail* (stripped to what a designer would sketch).
- **Benchmark:** published full LCAs of the same products, for rank-agreement checks.
- **Assessors:** two real, independent, LCA-competent people. Whether this activity needs ethics review is a local institutional question. Assessors are research staff, not subjects, but this must be confirmed.
- **Optional:** published concept sketches from prior ideation studies, if openly licensed. None were identified and verified in this gate.
- **Synthetic concepts:** usable only to debug the template and calculation tool. They must be labelled SYNTHETIC and are **not evidence** that the protocol works.

## 4. What this gate did not do

- No assessment was performed and no agreement was simulated.
- No inventory value was entered.
- No task was selected.

The endpoint is **specified, not validated**.
