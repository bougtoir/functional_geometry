# 04 — Novelty Boundary

## 1. Tested combination

**Brief framing level × bounded design search × functional equivalence × independently assessed environmental performance.**

| Component | Already tested? | Closest evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Framing / information manipulation before ideation | **Yes, many times** | Collado-Ruiz 2010 (information type); Liao & MacDonald 2021 (priming); She & MacDonald 2013; Hu et al. 2021 (problem statement); Ruiz-Pastor et al. 2020 (guided questions); Linsey et al. 2010 (examples/defixation) |
| Bounded search (fixed time) | **Yes** (standard in fixation experiments) | Linsey 2010; Hu 2021; Ruiz-Pastor 2020 |
| Environmental quality of generated ideas as outcome | **Yes, via expert ratings, rubrics or counts** | Liao & MacDonald 2021 (expert relevance rating); Greeley 2025 (rubric); Ruiz-Pastor 2020 (circularity count); Faludi 2019 (category counts) |
| Quantitative lifecycle model applied to experiment-generated concepts | **Yes, but economic, not environmental** | Cardin et al. 2013 |
| Screening LCA of early concepts | **Yes, as a design method, not as an experimental endpoint** | Bohm 2010; Devanathan 2010; Kota & Chakrabarti 2010; Arlitt et al. 2016 (JMD, doi:10.1115/1.4035431, AB) |
| Framing **level** specifically material vs form vs function | **Not found** | — |
| Functional-equivalence gate with failure kept as a separate outcome | **Not found** in environmental ideation experiments | — |
| Endpoint = impact per functional unit of the participant's **final selected** concept, assessed blind by ≥2 assessors with reliability reported | **Not found** | Faludi 2020 explicitly declined objective assessment |

Searched: OpenAlex, Crossref, citation networks of 4 seeds, plus user-provided full texts (Collado-Ruiz 2010, Ross et al. 2022, Collado-Ruiz et al. 2012). **Not** searched: Scopus, WoS, Google Scholar. Collado-Ruiz 2010 was read in full: no environmental outcome, and the authors name it as future work (p. 494).

## 2. Does E3 add a meaningful finding beyond established fixation research?

What is **already established** and cannot be claimed:
- Information, examples, priming and problem wording change idea features (fixation literature).
- Sustainability-oriented interventions change the *rated* environmental relevance of ideas (Liao & MacDonald 2021) or leave a circularity score unchanged (Ruiz-Pastor 2020, null).
- Concept-stage LCA is feasible in principle (Bohm 2010).

What would be **new**, if the endpoint is validated:
1. **Magnitude, not relevance.** Existing outcomes measure whether ideas are *about* environmental impact (ratings, counts). E3 would estimate *how much* impact per functional unit the selected concept implies, with uncertainty. This separates "more environmental-sounding ideas" from "lower-impact delivered function". The two can diverge: Ruiz-Pastor et al. 2021 (RED, FT-P) report novelty rising with motivation and affinity while circularity did not rise (non-significant).
2. **Functional failure as an outcome.** A framing that yields low-material but non-functional concepts would be scored as worse, not better. Expert relevance ratings cannot detect this.
3. **Selection, not generation.** Scoring the final selected concept connects to decisions; idea pools do not.
4. **Framing level as a variable.** Material vs form vs function framing is the specific lever ecodesign practice uses (substitution vs redesign vs function innovation; Ceschin & Gaziulusoy 2016). Its environmental consequence under a fixed budget has not been measured.

## 3. Boundary verdict

- **Conceptual novelty: none** (prior gate).
- **Manipulation novelty: low.** Framing/priming manipulations are standard; only the specific *level* contrast is new.
- **Endpoint novelty: moderate.** It is the only substantive contribution: a blinded, reliability-tested, functional-unit screening-LCA endpoint with a functional-equivalence gate for experiment-generated concepts. It is an *extension* of Liao & MacDonald 2021, Greeley 2025, Ruiz-Pastor 2020 and Cardin 2013, not a new paradigm.
- **Empirical novelty: survives narrowly**, conditional on:
  (i) ~~Collado-Ruiz 2010 full text showing no environmental-magnitude outcome~~: **satisfied** (FT, pp. 488, 494);
  (ii) a Scopus/WoS search finding no equivalent study, including published results of the Experiment 1/2 announced in Collado-Ruiz et al. 2012 (CIIP): **open**;
  (iii) ~~Ross et al. full text showing no LCA-type endpoint~~: **satisfied** (FT; counts and judge ratings only).
- **If the endpoint fails reliability, nothing novel remains.** The study would collapse to a replication of rating-based studies.

Framing the contribution honestly: *"We test whether the framing level of an environmental brief changes the functional-unit environmental impact, and the functional adequacy, of the concepts designers select, using a pre-validated blinded screening-LCA endpoint."* Not: *"function-first design is better."*
