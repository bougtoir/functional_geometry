# 25 — Final Novelty Boundary

**Not sufficient novelty claims** (all established):
- function before form;
- material substitution can mislead;
- framing affects creativity;
- information affects ideation (Collado-Ruiz 2010);
- a broader design space gives more alternatives;
- design decisions can be counted (Ross 2022);
- concepts can receive sustainability ratings (Liao 2021; Greeley 2025);
- brief/goal emphasis can be manipulated and environmental quality rated:
  - Ruiz-Pastor 2024: null, circularity measured blind;
  - Alzayed 2025: null, full text verified (22). It already scores **one final idea per participant** with a rubric rating, so "final selected concept" alone is not a differentiator.

**Surviving candidate:** the effect of brief-framing emphasis on *independently assessed impact per successful functional unit* of the *final selected* concept, with functional failure and assessment uncertainty handled separately.

| Criterion | Assessment |
|---|---|
| 1 Genuinely unanswered | **Pending.** Not found in OpenAlex, Crossref, citation tracing or the Collado line (23). Alzayed 2025 (FT) uses a rubric rating, not LCA. Scopus/WoS not searched |
| 2 Scientifically consequential | **Moderate.** It tests whether the content changes reported so far, which are mostly null at the rating level, correspond to any delivered-impact difference. A null result under a validated magnitude endpoint is informative mainly *methodologically* |
| 3 Empirically identifiable | **Yes in design** (O2 ITT plus hierarchical outcome; 17, 18, 31). It depends on the endpoint not being dominated by assumptions (30) |
| 4 Feasible with available resources | **Not yet.** It needs licensed inventory or verified open data, two or three LCA-competent assessors, and participants with ethics approval |
| 5 Sufficient for an original article | **Only if the endpoint is validated.** A rating-based version would be insufficient. The endpoint-validation pilot may itself be the stronger, earlier publishable unit |

**Narrowest surviving claim:** "In a randomised O2 experiment, does brief emphasis change the functional adequacy and the screening-LCA impact per functional unit of selected concepts, beyond what rating-based studies have reported?" The status is **pending verification**, not established novelty.
