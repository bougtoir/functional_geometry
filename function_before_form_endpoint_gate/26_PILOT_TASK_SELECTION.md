# 26 — Pilot Task Selection (preparation only)

Criteria:
- multiple plausible realisations;
- documented alternatives;
- specifiable FU;
- published LCA benchmarks;
- obtainable data;
- comparable boundaries;
- limited dependence on speculative behaviour.

Toilet paper was not considered. Tasks were *not* chosen for favouring any arm.

| Task | FU (draft) | Realisations | Published benchmarks (verified to exist; OA status) | Main weakness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TA — Single beverage serving (selected)** | Serving one 250 mL (or 473 mL) beverage at the point of sale for N servings | Single-use paper/plastic/bio cups; reusable cups (hand- or dish-washed); deposit/return systems; customer-owned tumblers; dispenser/in-store service | Potting & van der Harst 2015, *IJLCA*, doi:10.1007/s11367-015-0914-7 (OA); Cottafava et al. 2020, *SPC*, doi:10.1016/j.spc.2020.11.002 (OA; break-even for deposit return); Lee et al. 2025, *EER*, doi:10.4491/eer.2024.722 (OA, read: FU 113 cups/yr × 473 mL; reuse rate "significantly influenced the results") | Strongly reuse- and washing-dependent |
| **TB — Single take-away meal containment (selected)** | Containing and delivering one take-away meal portion from kitchen to consumer for N meals | Single-use containers by material; reusable containers with return; deposit systems; shared tableware; edible or minimal packaging | Gallego-Schmid et al. 2018, *STOTEN*, doi:10.1016/j.scitotenv.2018.02.128 (OA AAM); Yadav et al. 2024, *JCP*, doi:10.1016/j.jclepro.2024.141529 (OA; 10/30/100-use scenarios; ecoinvent 3.8); Aggarwal 2024, *CESYS*, doi:10.1016/j.cesys.2024.100223 (OA; 18 impact categories); Zhou et al. 2020, *Nature Food*, doi:10.1038/s43016-020-00145-0 (OA repository) | Return rates, food-safety and hygiene requirements |
| TC — E-commerce shipment protection (reserve) | Protecting and delivering one parcel of defined goods with damage rate ≤ x | Boxes, mailers, reusable shipping packaging, protective-system redesign | González-Romero et al. 2024, *JCP*, doi:10.1016/j.jclepro.2024.143738 (OA) | Logistics-dominated; less designer control |

Reviews of variability: Pålsson & Olsson 2023, *PTS*, doi:10.1002/pts.2722 (OA; 91 papers, "different conclusions"); Coelho et al. 2020, *RCR X*, doi:10.1016/j.rcrx.2020.100037 (OA); Caspers et al. 2023, *Sustainability*, doi:10.3390/su15054315 (consumer behaviour in takeaway LCAs).

**Selection: TA and TB**, with TC as reserve. Both selected tasks are **reuse-dominated**. The published reviews report divergent conclusions, which is exactly the assumption-dominance risk (30). That risk is accepted as a stress test of the endpoint: if the endpoint cannot rank concepts in these tasks, it is not fit for purpose.

Benchmarks were checked only for existence and OA status, plus abstract or partial text. FU compatibility and inventory transparency are **not yet verified** (27).
